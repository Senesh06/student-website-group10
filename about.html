About Us

The University of Ruhuna Canteen serves the students, academic staff and non-academic staff of the University of Ruhuna at Wellamadama, Matara. We believe good food helps students learn better, so we cook fresh meals every day at fair prices.

About the University of Ruhuna

The University of Ruhuna began in 1978 as Ruhuna University College and became a full university in 1984. It serves the Southern Province of Sri Lanka and its main premises are at Wellamadama, Matara.

Visit the official website: www.ruh.ac.lk
